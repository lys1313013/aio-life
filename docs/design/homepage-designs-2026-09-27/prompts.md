Below, asymmetrical 12-column layout: primary 时迹 panel across left 7 columns with delicate 24h horizontal timeline and smaller empty ring; 待办 occupies right 5 columns as compact purposeful panel. Slim full-width quick navigation rail between sections. Bottom 3 aligned compact panels 闪念 / 运动 / 最近提交. Rounded corners around 14px, calm nuanced surfaces, immaculate spacing, understated premium productivity tool aesthetic. Keep all elements within screenshot.
